### 1. Classification based on Geographical Scope

#### A. LAN (Local Area Network)

- **Scope:** Limited area (Ek ghar, school lab, office building).
    
- **Connection:** Fast, usually Ethernet (Wired) ya Wi-Fi (Wireless).
    
- **Devices:** Hubs, Switches.
    
- **Key:** Jab computers physically paas hote hain aur high-speed communication chahiye hota hai.

#### B. MAN (Metropolitan Area Network)

- **Scope:** Ek pura city (Sheher) ya bada campus.
    
- **Connection:** Do ya do se zyada LANs ko aapas me jorna (e.g., ek supermarket ki city ki 4 branches ko jorna).
    
- **Devices:** Routers, Bridges.
    
- **Key:** Jab ek city ke alag-alag areas me baithe networks ko connect karna ho.
    
![[Pasted image 20260817111303.png]]
#### C. WAN (Wide Area Network)

- **Scope:** Country-wide ya Global (Duniya bhar mein).
    
- **Connection:** Bohot bade distance par data transfer. Internet khud ek WAN hai.
    
- **Devices:** Routers, complex intermediary devices.
    
- **Key:** Do alag deshon (e.g., Delhi se Australia) ke networks ko connect karna.

### 2. Networking ke New Trends (Modern Era)

Interviewers aksar aajkal ke trends ke baare mein puchte hain:

- **BYOD (Bring Your Own Device):** Employees ka apna laptop/phone office network me connect karke kaam karna.
    
- **Online Collaboration:** Zoom/Meet/Teams ka use karke duniya bhar se log ek meeting me jud sakte hain (No need to be in one location).
    
- **Cloud Computing:** Data ko apne local computer me rakhne ke bajaye, remote servers (e.g., Google Drive) par store karna. Yeh "On-demand availability" provide karta hai—tum duniya ke kisi bhi kone se apna data access kar sakte ho.

### Interview Perspective (MAANG Focus)

- **LAN vs WAN:** Interviewer pucha sakta hai "What is the key difference?" — Answer: Scope, Speed, aur Complexity. LAN bohot fast hota hai aur manage karna aasan hota hai, WAN slow aur complex hota hai.
    
- **Scalability & Cloud:** Cloud computing ko hamesha "Scalability" aur "Accessibility" ke context me samjhana. Companies aajkal servers khud banane ke bajaye cloud (AWS/Azure) use karti hain kyunki yeh manage karna aasan aur scalable hai.
